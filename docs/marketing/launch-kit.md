# PixelPear launch kit

Copy to paste into directory submissions, plus the Search Console checklist.
Lead with the free tools. Directories and communities accept and link free tools far more readily than paid products.

> Before pasting: confirm the prices below match production (they come from `drizzle/init.sql`): free trial with 3 credits, then one-time $9 / $19 / $49 lifetime. No subscriptions.

## Reusable copy

**Name:** PixelPear

**URL to submit:** `https://pixelpear.io/tools/ats-resume-checker` (where a directory wants one URL for a free tool) or `https://pixelpear.io` (where it wants the product)

**Tagline (≤60 chars)**
- Free ATS resume checker built for international students
- Alt: Get your resume past ATS, and past US recruiters

**Short description (≤160 chars)**
> Free ATS resume checker and keyword matcher. Flags the US resume mistakes international students make. Runs in your browser; your resume is never uploaded.

**Long description (~120 words)**
> PixelPear helps job seekers, especially international students applying in the US, get their resumes read.
>
> The free ATS Resume Checker scores your resume on what applicant tracking systems and US recruiters look for: readable text, contact details, standard headings, quantified bullets and strong verbs. It also catches habits that are normal elsewhere but hurt you in the US: photos, birth dates, marital status, Chinese characters, non-US phone numbers, 100-point GPAs and "2023.06"-style dates.
>
> The free Keyword Matcher compares your resume to a job posting and shows which required skills are missing.
>
> Both run entirely in your browser. No sign-up, and your resume never leaves your device. When you're ready, PixelPear's AI tailors your resume to each job and exports an ATS-friendly PDF.

**Categories / tags:** Resume, Career, Job search, ATS, HR, Productivity, AI, Students

**Pricing field:** Freemium. Free tools unlimited, no sign-up; AI tailoring from $9 one-time.

**Screenshots to prepare (1270×760 or 1280×800):**
1. ATS checker result: score dial plus failed checks (use a demo resume with a photo, birth date and +86 phone number)
2. Keyword matcher: required / nice-to-have chips with found and missing
3. Before/after of an AI-tailored resume (demo data only)

Use demo resumes only, never a real user's resume.

> Hi Product Hunt! I built PixelPear after watching international students get rejected for resume reasons that had nothing to do with their skills: a photo in the header, a birth date, a +86 number, a GPA of "88" that US recruiters read as a typo.

## 6. Show HN (free)

Show HN is for things people can try right now, without a sign-up gate. The free tools qualify; the paid product page doesn't.

- **Title:** `Show HN: A resume checker that runs entirely in the browser`
- **URL:** `https://pixelpear.io/tools/ats-resume-checker`
- **Text (first comment):**

> I built a free ATS resume checker and keyword matcher. All parsing runs client-side: pdf.js for PDFs, and a ~60-line zip reader with DecompressionStream for .docx, so nothing is uploaded and it costs nothing to run.
>
> The checks are rules, not an LLM: section headings, contact info, quantified bullets, weak verbs, and a set aimed at international students, who often follow conventions that hurt them with US employers (photos, birth dates, CJK characters, 100-point GPAs, dotted dates).
>
> The keyword matcher pulls skills from a job description with a dictionary plus acronym and n-gram heuristics, then weights required skills double. It's rough, and I'd love examples of postings where it picks the wrong terms.

- **Rules:** a plain title with no hype words or exclamation marks. Don't ask anyone to upvote. Stay in the thread for the first few hours to answer questions.
- **Timing:** weekday mornings, US Eastern time.

## Search Console checklist

**Today:**
1. search.google.com/search-console → property `pixelpear.io`
2. **Sitemaps** → resubmit `https://pixelpear.io/sitemap.xml` (it now lists 17 URLs)
3. **URL Inspection** → paste each URL → **Request indexing**:
   - https://pixelpear.io/tools/ats-resume-checker
   - https://pixelpear.io/tools/resume-keyword-matcher
   - https://pixelpear.io/blog/us-resume-format-for-international-students
   - https://pixelpear.io/blog/translate-chinese-resume-to-english
   - https://pixelpear.io/blog/f1-opt-resume-work-authorization
   - https://pixelpear.io/blog/resume-for-international-students-no-us-experience

**Weekly (Monday, 10 minutes):**
1. **Performance → Search results**, last 28 days, tick **Average position**
2. **Queries** tab, sorted by impressions:
   - Position 8–20 with impressions → improve that page: add a section that answers the query directly, use the query wording in an H2, link to it from other posts
   - Lots of impressions but under 1% CTR → rewrite that page's title and description in `src/lib/seo-metadata.ts` or the post header
3. **Pages** tab: which pages get impressions at all. Pages with zero after 4 weeks may not be indexed; check them in URL Inspection.
4. Note any search you didn't expect. Each one is an idea for a new post.

Data lags 2–3 days. New pages usually take 1–4 weeks to show up.
